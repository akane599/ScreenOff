At a natural pause, ask what would have made the work easier, and whether a concrete weakness earns one improvement. Keep what works; no change for novelty. First check whether the project, stdlib, platform, installed plugins, skills, rules or instruments already cover it, and improve the installed capability before proposing a parallel one. Offer `/quartermaster:resupply` only with user approval; each change needs its own approval.
- **A claim you couldn't check** may need a measurement; improve an existing instrument before building one.
- **A multi-step task done by hand** may belong in a skill (skill-creator).
- **Something re-derived** may belong in a codebase map doc or a CLAUDE.md line.
- **A convention you had to be told** may belong in a tightly-scoped live rule.
If existing capabilities hold up, say so.
